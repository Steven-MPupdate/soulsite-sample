Pain Points They Solve

  • Heart-led writers who have a story inside them but cannot get it finished and published
  • Writers overwhelmed by the whole publishing process, from outlining to book cover to ISBN to marketing
  • Aspiring authors who keep procrastinating or deprioritizing their book until years pass without action
  • Writers who burn out trying to navigate editing, formatting, and promotion entirely on their own

Why They Do It

Alex created The Open Page because the first book was a lonely, expensive scramble to figure out alone. Between a full-time job and a young family, Alex spent years finishing a first manuscript, learning the hard way how outlining, editing, and publishing actually fit together. After the bestseller The Quiet Compass, Alex built a community and a professional team so other writers would not have to piece the process together on their own.

The Trials

For years Alex wrote in stolen hours, early mornings and late nights, and never quite finished. Three abandoned drafts and a folder of unsent query letters piled up. The first real book was self-published at considerable personal cost, then stalled with a publisher that overpromised and underdelivered, a slow and discouraging education in how the industry really works.

The Turning Point

After sharing early chapters in a small writers' group, Alex kept hearing the same question: why isn't this finished yet? That question landed. Alex committed to a repeatable process, published the international bestseller The Quiet Compass, and realized that same process could help other writers skip years of trial and error, which led directly to founding The Open Page.

The Vision

Alex is finishing a new novel and a series of craft workshops that show writers different ways to tell a story. The broader vision is to grow The Open Page so dedicated writers everywhere can move from idea to published book in as little as six months, supported by a full professional team.

Who They Serve

Dedicated, heart-led writers who already know they have a story to share but need guidance on the who, the where, and the how. Her ideal members are committed, not on the fence, and simply need a clear process, professional support, and community to move from idea to finished, published book. She helps with memoir, transformational, fiction, and fantasy books, and does not work on nonfiction.

What They Offer

  • A free writing community with weekly story challenges, prompts, and craft exercises
  • Page One Method coaching, guiding you from outline to published book in a six-month framework
  • A full professional team for editing, formatting, cover design, ISBN, synopsis, and author bio
