Meet Alex

Alex Rivers is a bestselling author and founder of The Open Page. After years of writing in the cracks of a busy life, Alex learned to turn a tangled first draft into a finished book, published the bestseller The Quiet Compass, and built a community and professional team so other writers would not have to face the publishing journey alone.

She helps with memoir, transformational, fiction, and fantasy books, guiding committed writers from idea to finished, published book.
